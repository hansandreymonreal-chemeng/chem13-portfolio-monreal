AI USE DISCLOSURE

Activity: One Molecule, Four Representations: From Molecular Formula to Bond-Line Structure

Artificial intelligence (ChatGPT) was used as a supplementary learning tool throughout the completion of this activity. It assisted in explaining the concepts and requirements of molecular formulas, Lewis structures, condensed structural formulas, and bond-line structures. It also provided step-by-step guidance for constructing the assigned molecules using Ketcher, including identifying atom connectivity, understanding implicit carbon and hydrogen atoms, placing heteroatoms, and representing double bonds and ring structures.

AI assistance was also used to clarify the structural representations of the ten assigned compounds: propane, ethanol, acetone, acetic acid, ethyl acetate, cyclohexane, cyclohexanone, acetaminophen, aspirin, and caffeine. Additional guidance was requested when difficulties arose in drawing certain structures, particularly the more complex fused-ring structures.

All AI-provided explanations and structural guidance were reviewed and cross-checked against other online chemistry references and websites to verify their accuracy and consistency with accepted chemical structures. The Ketcher structures were prepared and reviewed by the student, who remains responsible for the correctness and final presentation of the submitted work.

AI was used for educational guidance and clarification, not as a replacement for the student's own understanding, verification, and responsibility for the completed activity.
